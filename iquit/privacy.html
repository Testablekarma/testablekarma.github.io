Privacy Policy

Bracken Coach
Effective Date: September 3, 2026

Privacy Summary:

  • Your quit plan, diary entries, photos, and health data stay on your device — the coach never transmits them anywhere
  • The on-device coach runs entirely on Apple's Foundation Models; no third-party AI service is involved, and no account is needed to use it
  • The optional Community feature is different: it uses an anonymous account and a third-party service (Google Firebase) to share milestones with other users — see Section 2
  • We use no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs of any kind
  • We do not sell your personal information

This Privacy Policy describes how Mark Webb ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Bracken Coach mobile application ("App"). The App has two clearly separate parts with different privacy properties: your private coach, diary and health data (never transmitted), and the optional, anonymous Community feature (which does use a server). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Your Private Data — Coach, Diary, and Health

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Quit plan: your quit date, vaping profile (device type, nicotine strength, cost), and optional nicotine replacement therapy (NRT) details.
  • Onboarding conversation: why you're quitting, what you've tried before, and when it's hardest for you, in your own words. This is stored verbatim — the App never rewrites or summarizes what you write.
  • Diary entries: mood, notes, whether a craving occurred, and any photos you choose to attach.

All of this is stored locally on your device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData storage. None of it requires an account, and none of it is ever transmitted anywhere — not to us, not to Apple, and not to any third party.

1.2 The On-Device Coach

The App's coach runs on Apple's Foundation Models framework, entirely on your device. Anything you type to the coach, and anything it reads from your diary, quit plan, or health data to respond, is processed on-device and is never sent to us, to Apple, or to any third-party AI service. On devices where on-device AI isn't available, the coach falls back to a deterministic, non-AI set of responses — this fallback is also entirely on-device.

If anything you write to the coach suggests you may be in crisis, the App detects this on-device, before your text would otherwise reach the model, and shows you crisis support resources instead.

1.3 Health Data

If you grant permission, the App reads resting heart rate, heart rate variability, respiratory rate, VO2 max, and sleep data from Apple Health (most of which requires a paired Apple Watch) to show how your body has changed since you quit, and so the coach can mention it. This data is read on-device only, is never transmitted anywhere, and is never written back to Apple Health by the App. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings.

1.4 Camera and Photo Library

If you choose to attach a photo to a diary entry, the App accesses your camera or photo library only for that purpose, with your permission. Photos stay on your device as part of your diary entry and are never uploaded.

1.5 Notifications

The App schedules local notifications on your device (for example, at milestones in your quit journey). These are scheduled entirely on-device — the App does not use push notifications for this and does not operate a server to send them.

2. The Optional Community Feature

Community is a separate, optional feature for anonymously sharing quit milestones. Unlike everything in Section 1, Community does use a server — specifically Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, and Cloud Functions) — and this section describes exactly what that involves.

2.1 What Community Collects

  • An anonymous account. The first time you open Community, the App creates an anonymous Firebase account for you and generates a random handle and avatar (for example, "Quiet Forest 42") — never your name, email, or any other App data.
  • Milestone posts. If you choose to share a milestone, the post includes the milestone type and value (for example, a streak length or amount saved), and an optional caption of up to 140 characters that you write. Captions are filtered before publication and may not contain contact information, links, or disallowed language.
  • Reactions, replies, and reports you send to other people's posts, and any reports other users send about your posts.

2.2 How Community Data Is Used and Shared

Milestone posts, your anonymous handle and avatar, and your reactions and replies are visible to other users of Community. Reports are visible only to us, for moderation. We do not sell this data, and we do not use it for advertising. Google Firebase processes this data on our behalf as our infrastructure provider; see Google's Firebase privacy documentation for how Firebase itself handles data.

2.3 Deleting Your Community Data

You can delete an individual post at any time from within Community. You can also delete your entire Community account — from Settings, choose "Delete my community account" — which removes your profile, every post you've made, and every reaction, reply, or report you've left on other people's posts, and deletes the underlying anonymous account. This is separate from, and does not affect, the private data described in Section 1.

Community is entirely optional. If you never open it, none of this section applies to you, and no Community account is ever created.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

Bracken Coach does not use, and does not contain code for:

  • Analytics or usage-tracking SDKs
  • Advertising networks or ad identifiers
  • Crash-reporting or error-tracking services
  • Third-party AI services (the coach is on-device only)

Outside of the optional Community feature described in Section 2, the App makes no network requests to our servers or anyone else's, and we never collect your name, email address, or any identifying information.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your private data (Section 1) is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework, on-device, protected by your device's own security (passcode, Face ID/Touch ID, and iOS's data protection). We do not store a copy of it anywhere else. If you delete the App, this data is deleted with it. Community data (Section 2) is stored on Google Firebase's infrastructure until you delete it as described in 2.3.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your information. Your private data (Section 1) is never shared because it is never transmitted anywhere. Community data (Section 2) is shared only in the ways described in that section — with other Community users, and with Google Firebase as our infrastructure provider.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Edit or delete individual diary entries at any time in the App
  • Delete all App data by deleting the App from your device
  • Delete an individual Community post, or your whole Community account, from within the App (see 2.3)
  • Revoke camera, photo library, or Health access at any time in iOS Settings

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new Privacy Policy here and update the "Effective Date" above. Your continued use of the App after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
