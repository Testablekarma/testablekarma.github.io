Privacy Policy

iQuit: Vape Free Tracker
Effective Date: July 19, 2026

Privacy Summary:

  • All your data — quit date, diary entries, photos, progress — stays on your device
  • iQuit has no server, no account system, and does not transmit your data anywhere
  • We use no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs of any kind
  • We do not sell or share your personal information — there is nothing to share

This Privacy Policy describes how Mark Webb ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the iQuit mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information the App Stores

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Quit plan: your quit date, vaping profile (device type, nicotine strength, cost), and optional nicotine replacement therapy (NRT) details.
  • Diary entries: mood, notes, whether a craving occurred, and any photos you choose to attach.
  • Your reasons for quitting and any preferences you set within the App.

All of this is stored locally on your device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData storage. The App does not require an account, does not have a server, and does not transmit this information anywhere.

1.2 Camera and Photo Library

If you choose to attach a photo to a diary entry, the App accesses your camera or photo library only for that purpose, with your permission. Photos stay on your device as part of your diary entry and are never uploaded.

1.3 Notifications

The App schedules local notifications on your device (for example, at milestones in your quit journey). These are scheduled entirely on-device — the App does not use push notifications and does not operate a server to send them.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

iQuit does not use, and does not contain code for:

  • Analytics or usage-tracking SDKs
  • Advertising networks or ad identifiers
  • Crash-reporting or error-tracking services
  • Third-party AI services
  • Any network requests to our servers or anyone else's

There is no account system, so we never collect your name, email address, or any identifying information through the App itself.

3. How We Use Information

Because everything stays on your device, the only "use" of your information is to power the App's own features for you — showing your streak, health milestones, diary history, and craving charts. We never see this data, and we cannot access it.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework, on-device, protected by your device's own security (passcode, Face ID/Touch ID, and iOS's data protection). We do not store a copy of your data anywhere else. If you delete the App, your data is deleted with it.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not share, sell, or transmit your information to anyone, because the App does not transmit your information anywhere. The only exception is if required by law — but since we do not hold your data on any server, we would have nothing to provide.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Because your data lives entirely on your device, you are always in full control of it:

  • Edit or delete individual diary entries at any time in the App
  • Delete all App data by deleting the App from your device
  • Revoke camera or photo library access at any time in iOS Settings

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children — in fact, we do not collect personal information from anyone, as described above.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new Privacy Policy here and update the "Effective Date" above. Your continued use of the App after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
