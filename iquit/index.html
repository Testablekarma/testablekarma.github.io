Why Vapor Coach

A coach, not a form

From your first launch, the coach asks why you're quitting — in your own words — then sets one action each day and follows up the next on how it went.

Private by construction

The coach runs on Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Nothing you tell it, your diary, or your health data ever leaves your phone — no account, no third-party AI.

Ride out a craving

Log cravings and mood in your diary, spot your triggers, and use a guided breathing exercise when a craving hits.

Privacy

Your coach, diary, and health data never leave your device — there's no server for any of it. An optional Community feature lets you share milestones anonymously, which does use a server; read the full policy here.
