Delete your DAIS account and data

DAIS: Domestic Abuse Diary (The Daisy Chain Project)
Last updated: 19 November 2026

Deleting your account permanently removes your account and every diary entry, photo and video. It cannot be undone. Export your diary first if you need a copy.

Option 1: delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open DAIS and enter your passcode.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account and data.
  4. Confirm with Delete everything.

This deletes your data from our server and from your phone straight away.

Option 2: ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the app or your phone, email info@thedaisychainproject.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my DAIS account". We will delete your account and data and confirm by email. For your safety, we will only reply to the registered address.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, email address and login).
  • All diary entries: date, location, events and feelings.
  • All photos and videos attached to entries, with their captions.
  • The copy of your diary and your passcode stored on your phone.

What we may keep

Anonymous crash reports and usage statistics are not linked to your diary and are not deleted individually. Sentry crash reports and Firebase Analytics data are kept for the retention periods set by those providers. If you exported your diary and shared it with someone (for example the police or a solicitor), we cannot delete their copy.

Staying safe

Deleting your account does not remove the app from your phone. If you are worried someone may see it, uninstall it and clear your recent apps. If you are in immediate danger, call 999.
