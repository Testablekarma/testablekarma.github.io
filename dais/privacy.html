Privacy Policy

DAIS: Domestic Abuse Diary (The Daisy Chain Project)
Effective date: 19 November 2026

DAIS is a private diary. We collect only what the app needs to work, we do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising. You can delete your account and all your data at any time (see how to delete your account).

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and your password (stored in hashed form).
  • Your diary: entries you write (date, location, events, feelings) and any photos or videos you attach, with captions.
  • Passcode: stored only on your phone, never sent to us.
  • Crash reports: technical details if the app crashes, via Sentry. We do not attach screenshots of your screen. These may include a technical user ID.
  • Usage statistics: a small number of anonymous events (for example when a law summary is opened), via Firebase Analytics.

How we use it

  • To provide the diary and keep it backed up to your account.
  • To let you log in and reset your password.
  • To find and fix crashes and understand which features are used.

Who processes it

Your account and diary are stored with our hosting provider, Back4app, over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and are accessible only to your account. Sentry and Google (Firebase) process crash and usage data for us. These providers act on our behalf and may not use the data for their own purposes. We share your diary with other people only if you choose to export it and send it yourself.

Photos, video and permissions

The app asks to use your camera and photo library only when you add photos or videos to an entry, and the phone dialler only for the quick-dial 999 button (which asks you to confirm first).

How long we keep it

Until you delete an entry or your account. Deleting your account removes your account and diary from our server and from your phone.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data. Most of this can be done in the app. For anything else, or to complain about how we handle your data, email info@thedaisychainproject.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

DAIS is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
