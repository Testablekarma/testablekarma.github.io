Privacy Policy

Enclosr · Effective November 7, 2026

In short

  • Your logs stay on your devices and, if iCloud is on for Enclosr, in your own private iCloud account. Enclosr has no account and no server of ours.
  • We collect no personal information and cannot see your data. The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting or third-party code.
  • We do not sell, share or track your data.

This policy explains how Mark Webb ("we", "us") handles information in the Enclosr app ("the App"), a habitat log for aquariums, vivariums and terrariums.

1. What the App stores

When you use Enclosr you create records: habitats, parameter readings, animals, weight entries, journal notes, tasks, and photos you choose to attach. These are stored in the App's database on your iPhone or iPad. Enclosr's home-screen widget, its Control Centre control and its Apple Watch app read from that data on your own devices. We never receive any of it.

2. iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, the App syncs your habitats, readings, animals, tasks, journal entries and photos between your own devices using Apple's CloudKit, in your private iCloud database. It uses your iCloud storage. The data is held by Apple under your Apple Account; we have no access to it, and nothing is sent to any other server. You can turn it off for Enclosr in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud.

3. What else leaves your device

Nothing else leaves your device unless you send it yourself:

  • Backups and CSV exports are files you choose to save or share, using the iOS share and Files sheets. Where they go is up to you.
  • Device backups. If you use iCloud or computer backups of your device, Apple may include the App's data in them under your own Apple settings. We have no access to those backups.

4. Permissions

  • Camera — only to take photos you attach to your records. Photos are stored in the App and travel only through your own iCloud sync.
  • Photo library — to attach photos you pick, and to save photos you export. The App only sees the photos you select.
  • Notifications — for task reminders. They are scheduled on your device; no notification server of ours is involved.

5. Purchases

Enclosr Pro is offered as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription and a one-time lifetime purchase, all through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and manages the subscription. We do not receive your payment details or Apple Account. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles them.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

Delete a habitat to remove its records, or delete the App to remove everything it stores on that device. To remove the synced copy too, use iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Enclosr › Delete Data. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing further to request from us. You can revoke camera, photo or notification access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

8. Changes to this policy

If the App's handling of data changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships.

9. Contact
