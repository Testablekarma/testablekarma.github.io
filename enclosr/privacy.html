Privacy Policy

Enclosr · Effective October 1, 2026

In short

  • Everything you log stays on your device. Enclosr has no account and no server.
  • We collect no personal information, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code.
  • We do not sell, share or track your data.

This policy explains how Mark Webb ("we", "us") handles information in the Enclosr app ("the App"), a habitat log for aquariums, vivariums and terrariums.

1. What the App stores

When you use Enclosr you create records: habitats, parameter readings, animals, weight entries, journal notes, tasks, and photos you choose to attach. These are stored in the App's local database on your device, shared only with Enclosr's own home-screen widget and controls on that same device. We never receive them.

2. What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device unless you send it yourself:

  • Backups and CSV exports are files you choose to save or share, using the iOS share and Files sheets. Where they go is up to you.
  • Device backups. If you use iCloud or computer backups of your device, Apple may include the App's data in them under your own Apple settings. We have no access to those backups.

3. Permissions

  • Camera — only to take photos you attach to your records. Photos are stored in the App on your device.
  • Photo library — to attach photos you pick, and to save photos you export. The App only sees the photos you select.
  • Notifications — for task reminders. They are scheduled on your device; no notification server is involved.

4. Purchases

Enclosr Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and manages the subscription. We do not receive your payment details or Apple ID.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

6. Your choices

Because your data lives only on your device, you control it: delete a habitat to remove its records, or delete the App to remove everything it stores. Revoke camera, photo or notification access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

7. Changes to this policy

If the App's handling of data changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: apps.backflip.media@gmail.com.
