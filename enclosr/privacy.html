Privacy Policy

Enclosr · Effective November 18, 2026

In short

  • Your logs stay on your devices and, if iCloud is on for Enclosr, in your own private iCloud account. Enclosr has no account and no server of ours.
  • We collect no personal information and cannot see your data. The only things the App sends us are anonymous crash reports and anonymous usage statistics, described in section 3. It has no advertising and no tracking.
  • We do not sell, share or track your data.

This policy explains how Mark Webb ("we", "us") handles information in the Enclosr app ("the App"), a habitat log for aquariums, vivariums and terrariums.

1. What the App stores

When you use Enclosr you create records: habitats, parameter readings, animals, weight entries, journal notes, tasks, and photos you choose to attach. These are stored in the App's database on your iPhone or iPad. Enclosr's home-screen widget, its Control Centre control and its Apple Watch app read from that data on your own devices. We never receive any of it.

2. iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, the App syncs your habitats, readings, animals, tasks, journal entries and photos between your own devices using Apple's CloudKit, in your private iCloud database. It uses your iCloud storage. The data is held by Apple under your Apple Account; we have no access to it, and nothing is sent to any other server. You can turn it off for Enclosr in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud.

3. What else leaves your device

Apart from iCloud sync, the only things that leave your device are the two kinds of anonymous report below, plus anything you send yourself:

  • Crash reports. If Enclosr crashes or hits a serious error, it sends a report to Sentry (sentry.io), a crash-reporting service, hosted in the United States. A report contains technical details only: the App version, your device model and iOS version, a stack trace showing where the fault happened, and the time. It does not contain your habitats, readings, animals, notes or photos. It is not linked to you or your Apple Account, it does not include your name, device name or IP address, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. We use it only to find and fix bugs.
  • Usage statistics. Enclosr uses Firebase Analytics (Google) to count how the App is used, so we can see what works. It records a handful of events: the App being opened, a habitat being created (and whether it is an aquarium or a terrarium), readings being logged (and how many), the Pro screen being shown (and which feature prompted it), and a Pro purchase completing (and which plan). It never records habitat or animal names, notes, reading values, photos or dates you enter. Google also receives standard technical details with these events: the App version, device model, iOS version, language and a rough region. The events are tied to a random identifier created for the App on your device. It is not your name, Apple Account, advertising identifier or device identifier, it is not linked to you, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. Advertising features and personalised ads are switched off, and the advertising-identifier part of the SDK is not included. Deleting the App deletes that identifier. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles analytics data.
  • Backups and CSV exports are files you choose to save or share, using the iOS share and Files sheets. Where they go is up to you.
  • Device backups. If you use iCloud or computer backups of your device, Apple may include the App's data in them under your own Apple settings. We have no access to those backups.

4. Permissions

  • Camera — only to take photos you attach to your records. Photos are stored in the App and travel only through your own iCloud sync.
  • Photo library — to attach photos you pick, and to save photos you export. The App only sees the photos you select.
  • Notifications — for task reminders. They are scheduled on your device; no notification server of ours is involved.

5. Purchases

Enclosr Pro is offered as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription and a one-time lifetime purchase, all through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and manages the subscription. We do not receive your payment details or Apple Account. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles them.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

Delete a habitat to remove its records, or delete the App to remove everything it stores on that device. To remove the synced copy too, use iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Enclosr › Delete Data. Because we hold no copy of your logs, there is nothing further to request from us. Crash reports and usage statistics are anonymous and cannot be traced back to you. You can revoke camera, photo or notification access at any time in the iOS Settings app.

8. Changes to this policy

If the App's handling of data changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change ships.
