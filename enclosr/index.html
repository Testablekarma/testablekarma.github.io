Support

Enclosr is a habitat log for aquariums, vivariums and terrariums. If something isn't working or you have a question, you're in the right place.

Contact

Email apps.backflip.media@gmail.com and include your iPhone or iPad model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. Replies usually arrive within a couple of days.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device only. Enclosr has no account and no server, so your habitats, readings, animals and photos are never uploaded. That also means we can't recover data for you if the app is deleted — see the next question.

How do I move to a new phone, or keep a safe copy?

Enclosr Pro includes backup and restore. Open Settings › Data and choose Back up to save one file to Files, then use Restore from backup on the new device. CSV export of your readings is free for everyone.

My reminders aren't arriving.

Reminders are local notifications. Check that notifications are allowed for Enclosr in the iOS Settings app, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them. If a reminder still doesn't appear, email us the habitat and the task it belongs to.

What does the free version include?

Up to two habitats in any combination of aquariums and terrariums, 90 days of chart history, and 25 photos. Reminders and CSV export are always free. Enclosr Pro removes the limits.

How do I restore a purchase, or cancel my subscription?

Open the Pro screen in Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Subscriptions are managed by Apple: go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your device. We can't cancel or refund purchases for you; refunds are handled at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a habitat inside the app to remove it and its readings, or delete the app to remove everything it stores on the device. Nothing is held anywhere else.
