Support

Enclosr is a habitat log for aquariums, vivariums and terrariums. If something isn't working or you have a question, you're in the right place.

Contact

Email apps.backflip.media+enclosr@gmail.com and include your iPhone or iPad model, your iOS version, the Enclosr version (Settings, at the bottom), and what you were doing when the problem happened. Replies usually arrive within a couple of days.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone or iPad, and, if you are signed in to iCloud, synced between your own devices through your private iCloud. Sync is free. Enclosr has no account and no server of ours, so we cannot see your habitats, readings, animals or photos, and we cannot recover them for you.

Sync is not working.

Check that you are signed in to iCloud and that Enclosr is switched on in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud. Sync uses your iCloud storage, so a full iCloud account will pause it. Changes can take a few minutes to arrive on your other devices.

How do I keep a safe copy, or move to a new phone?

With iCloud on, a new device signed in to the same Apple Account picks your data up by itself. For a copy you control, Enclosr Pro includes backup and restore: open Settings › Data and choose Back up to save one file to Files, then Restore from backup on the other device. CSV export of your readings is free for everyone.

My reminders aren't arriving.

Reminders are local notifications. Check that notifications are allowed for Enclosr in the iOS Settings app, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them. If a reminder still doesn't appear, email us the habitat and the task it belongs to.

What does the free version include?

Up to three habitats in any combination of aquariums and terrariums, 90 days of chart history, and 25 photos. Reminders and CSV export are always free. Enclosr Pro removes the limits.

How do I restore a purchase, or cancel my subscription?

Open Settings › Pro and tap Restore purchase. This works for the monthly and yearly subscriptions and for the lifetime purchase, using the Apple Account that bought it. Subscriptions are managed by Apple: go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your device. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. We can't cancel or refund purchases for you; refunds are handled at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a habitat inside the app to remove it and its readings, or delete the app to remove everything it stores on that device. To remove the synced copy as well, go to iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Enclosr and choose Delete Data.
