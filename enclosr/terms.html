The app

Enclosr is a logbook for aquariums, vivariums and terrariums, published by PUBLISHER_NAME_PLACEHOLDER. On iOS, use of the app is also covered by Apple's standard licensed application end user licence agreement.

Free and Pro

The free plan covers up to three habitats, 90 days of chart history and 25 photos. Reminders and CSV export are free on every habitat. Enclosr Pro removes those limits and adds backup and restore. Pro is offered as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription (which may begin with a free trial) and a one-time lifetime purchase. The price is shown in the app before you buy.

Subscriptions

Payment is taken by your app store account (the App Store or Google Play) when you confirm the purchase. A subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. You can manage or cancel it in your store account settings; deleting the app does not cancel it. If a free trial is offered and you do not cancel before it ends, the subscription starts and you are charged. Refunds are handled by the store under its own policy. The lifetime purchase is paid once and does not renew. Use Restore purchase in Settings › Pro to recover a purchase on a new device.
